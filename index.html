About me

I’m a B.Tech Computer Science student at Maharaja Surajmal Institute of Technology, New Delhi, graduating in 2026. My work sits at the intersection of data analytics and applied AI — I like taking messy, real-world datasets and making them tell a clear story.

My flagship project is HireLens, an AI-powered interview simulator I built from scratch: it transcribes spoken answers with Whisper, scores them with TF-IDF semantics and audio features, and auto-generates PDF performance reports. I documented it as an IEEE-format research paper and presented it to an academic panel.

Previously, I worked in B2B client support at Acefone, resolving 20–25 tickets a day — which taught me to translate between business needs and technical fixes, a skill I bring to every analysis.
